This app includes practice questions from CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS by Amey Thakur.
本アプリには、Amey Thakur 氏の CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS に含まれる練習問題が含まれています。

Source / 出典: https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS (associate-foundations)

Included material / 含まれるもの:
- data/questions/practice.json (Q001-Q035): practice-questions.md, questions 1-35
- data/questions/mock1.json (Q036-Q050): mock-exam-1.md, questions 1-15
- data/questions/mock2.json (Q051-Q065): mock-exam-2.md, questions 1-15
- data/questions/mock3.json (Q066-Q080): mock-exam-3.md, questions 1-15
- public/questions.json (the merged file served by the app / アプリが読み込む結合済みファイル)

Modifications / 変更点:
- Japanese translations were added. The English question text, choices, and answers are unchanged.
  日本語訳を追加しました。英語の問題文・選択肢・正解は変更していません。
- For mock exams, explanations for some incorrect choices were added, marked with
  "[Supplement]" / "【補足】".
  模擬試験の一部の問題に、誤答の説明を補足として追加しました（"[Supplement]" / "【補足】" と表示）。
